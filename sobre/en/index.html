Lista de Compras

Shop together: shared lists that update in real time.

Open the app How it works Help

Web app (PWA) for anyone sharing the household shopping. Works in the browser and installs on your phone, no app store needed. The app comes in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

What it does

Create a list, invite the people you shop with by e-mail, and that is it: every item checked, added or removed shows up instantly for everyone. Add each item's price and keep track of what the shopping will cost.

Share by e-mail

Invite family or friends using the e-mail of their account. Only invited people can see the list.

Real time

Checked the milk off? Everyone sees it right away, without reloading the page.

Price and total

Enter each item's price and see every list's total, right on the cards of the home screen.

By category

Items are grouped into Produce, Butcher, Bakery and more, and the app suggests the category from the name.

Check it offline

At the supermarket with no signal, you can still see the last saved version of the list.

Installs like an app

Add it to your phone's home screen and open it like any other app.

What it looks like

App screens with sample data.

How it works

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your name, e-mail and a password of at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. Confirm the e-mail you receive and sign in.

  2. Create a list

    Give it a name, like "Monthly groceries", and tap "Create".

  3. Add the items

    Type the name, and the quantity and price if you like. The category is suggested automatically, and you can change it.

  4. Share

    Tap the people icon, enter the e-mail of who will join and tap "Share". That person needs to have an account already.

Install on your phone

Android (Chrome): open the app, tap the ⋮ menu and "Install app" or "Add to home screen".
iPhone (Safari): tap Share and "Add to Home Screen".

Each list is visible only to the people invited. The owner can remove members or delete the list, and each member can leave the list at any time.

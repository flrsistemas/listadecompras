Privacy Policy

Lista de Compras app · FLR Sistemas · Effective November 18, 2026

Lista de Compras keeps only what it needs to work.

Your name, your e-mail and the lists you create. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Only the people you invite can see your lists.

Who we are

Lista de Compras is developed and maintained by FLR Sistemas. For any question about this policy, write to contato@flrsistemas.com.br.

The data we collect

For the app to work, we need an account and the content you create in it:

DataWhat it is for
NameShown to you and to the people in your lists.
E-mailSigning in, confirming sign-up and receiving list invitations.
PasswordAuthentication. It is stored encrypted, and we cannot read it.
Lists, items, quantities, prices and categoriesThe content you write, shown to you and to the members of each list.
Members of each listControlling who can see and edit each list.

What the app does not do

  • It shows no advertising and uses no advertising identifier.
  • It uses no analytics, usage statistics or tracking.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or files.
  • It does not collect payment data.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

Who sees your lists

People you invite by e-mail can see the items, the prices and the name and e-mail of the other members of that list. People who are not in the list see nothing: access rules in the database ensure this. The owner can remove members or delete the list, and each member can leave at any time.

Third-party services

The app uses three services to work:

ServiceRoleWhat it receives
SupabaseStores the account and lists, handles sign-in and sends the confirmation e-mail.The data in the table above. Its servers may be located outside Brazil.
GitHub PagesHosts the site and the app.Visitors' IP address, as with any website.

Each follows its own privacy policy.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps in your device's browser your sign-in session, the language you chose and a copy of your lists, items and totals, so you can view them without internet. When you sign out or clear the browser data, that copy is removed from the device.

How long we keep it

We keep the data while your account exists. When a list is deleted, its items are deleted for every member. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time.

Security

The connection is protected by HTTPS, passwords are stored encrypted, and each person can read only the lists they take part in. No system is 100% secure, but we take care to reduce the risk.

Children

Lista de Compras is not directed at children.

Your rights

Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), and similar laws elsewhere, give you the right to confirm that we process your data, access it, correct it, ask for its deletion and portability. Inside the app you can see and edit your lists at any time. For other requests, or to delete your account, write to contato@flrsistemas.com.br from the registered e-mail. We reply to the same address.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data in a new way, this page will be updated before the change goes live, and the date at the top will change.
